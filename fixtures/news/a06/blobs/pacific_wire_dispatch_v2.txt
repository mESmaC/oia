PUBLISHED: 2026-10-16T03:40:00Z (SUPERSEDES VERSION 1 OF 2026-10-15T21:05:00Z)
DATELINE: HONOLULU (West Approach Channel 21.318N 158.132W and Offshore Warning Area W-188B, WGS84)

CORRECTION NOTICE: An earlier version of this dispatch misstated the number of vessels logged in the Barbers Point West Approach Channel due to a transcription error ("14-meter survey launch" read as "14 vessels"). The watch log recorded 4 vessels total (one 14-meter commercial survey launch, two harbor tugs, and one coastal barge). In addition, the offshore Navy range transit to Warning Area W-188B was a separately scheduled calibration exercise announced on Oct. 14, and the contractor who alleged a military payload recovery withdrew the claim after Port Authority enforcement records identified the craft as a private hydrographic survey launch with a failed AIS unit.

HONOLULU (PWS) -- Harbor pilotage at Barbers Point was held for 35 minutes Thursday morning while two assist tugs escorted an unlit 14-meter commercial survey launch clear of West Approach Channel Buoy #3, according to watch logs and Port Authority citations. Four vessels were in the approach sector during the hold. Separate U.S. Coast Guard notices confirm that naval support vessels transiting further offshore were conducting a pre-announced sensor calibration exercise in Warning Area W-188B unrelated to the harbor channel hold.
