3. Reported observations

Three written accounts were received between 15 and 22 March. Each describes a slow-moving light above the ridge line during the evening of 14 March. The accounts do not agree on the direction of travel.

The first account, from a resident on the eastern road, places the light moving from the north-west toward the south-east over approximately eight minutes, with no sound reported. The reported time was "about twenty to ten", local.
