Harbor Operations Held Near Barbers Point; Wire Dispatch Cites 14 Vessels

HONOLULU (PWS Reprint) -- Harbor operations west of Honolulu were disrupted Thursday morning after an unidentified surface craft triggered a channel hold near Barbers Point while naval range ships departed for offshore waters.

According to cooperative watch logs reviewed by Pacific Wire Service, 14 vessels were involved in the West Approach Channel hold near Buoy #3. Duty pilot Capt. M. Kealoha stated that harbor control held inbound pilotage for 35 minutes while the craft was escorted clear. An unnamed dockside contractor alleged a link to Warning Area W-188B, which authorities have not confirmed.
