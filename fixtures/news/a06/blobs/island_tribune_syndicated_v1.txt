HEADLINE: NAVAL BLOCKADE HALTS COMMERCIAL SHIPPING ACROSS PACIFIC CORRIDOR

By Tribune Wire Desk (Adapted from PWS-2026-1015-884)

Commercial shipping lanes across the Pacific corridor were effectively shut down Thursday after a naval blockade and a 14-vessel confrontation sealed the western approaches to Oahu.

In a dramatic statement, duty pilot Capt. M. Kealoha said "harbor control confirmed a full military lockdown" after an unidentified craft was intercepted near Buoy #3 during a classified sub-surface recovery mission in Warning Area W-188B.

With seven regional news articles published on harbor incidents this week compared to two articles last week, maritime crime and security breaches in the Oahu corridor have surged by 350%.
