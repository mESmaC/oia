- **Status:** Implemented as a standalone kernel in [`oia-news-claims`](../../crates/oia-news-claims/src/regional.rs) for one slice of [issue #20](https://github.com/mESmaC/oia/issues/20). The issue stays open.
- **Date:** 2026-11-04 UTC
- **Requirements:** [`NEWS-02`](../spec.md#44-news-regional-intelligence-and-claim-assessment); the separate-count part of [`GEO-03`](../spec.md#42-geographic-events-and-uap-investigations)
- **Acceptance scenario:** the regional-account subset of [`A06`](../delivery.md#a06-news-lineage-and-claim-assessment). This is not full end-to-end `A06` or `A19`.
- **Builds on:** [ADR 0005 (`D03`)](../adr/0005-d03-seed-corpus-and-news-sources.md), the [`A06` fixture](../../fixtures/news/a06/README.md), and the [claim-checking contract](../contracts/news-claim-checking.md) (`verify_corpus`)

## What it does

`regional::build_regional_account` takes a news corpus, runs the existing `verify_corpus` checks on it, and then builds an account of reported developments for one region and one UTC time window.

1. **Epistemic categories stay separate.** Each event candidate gets one `EpistemicCategory`: `Plan`, `Observation`, `AttributedStatement`, `Allegation`, `Arrest`, `Charge`, or `Judgment`. It also gets a `DevelopmentKind`: military movement, exercise, deployment, criminal activity, public-safety incident, transport disruption, or other. Categories are never merged or upgraded, and category counts are kept per event and per region.
2. **Movement does not establish purpose.** A `StatedPurposeInput` (intent, mission, or destination) is accepted only on a plan, attributed statement, allegation, or judgment, and only when it cites a passage in that candidate's own source record. The output `AttributedPurpose` names who stated it and why: `attribution_basis` is `NamedSpeaker` when the candidate gives a speaker, or `IssuingSource` when it does not. In the second case `attributed_to` is the publishing source (for a news article, the outlet), not an actor. The purpose keeps the entry's own category, so an alleged mission is still an allegation. An observation that reports movement and carries a purpose is rejected with `MovementPromotedToPurpose`. Any other category carrying a purpose is rejected with `PurposeNotAttributable`. Attributed statements and allegations without a speaker are rejected with `MissingAttribution`. The corpus-level `movement_establishes_intent_or_mission` guard in `verify_corpus` still applies.
3. **Grouping is an explicit input.** The caller assigns every candidate to a `ProposedEventInput` and names the `grouping_decision_id` it comes from, for example a cluster revision. The kernel does not cluster automatically and does not re-implement the reversible ledger in [`oia-query::geo_workspace`](../../crates/oia-query/src/geo_workspace.rs). Events may list extra member versions that have no typed candidate, such as syndicated reprints or corrected versions.
4. **Independence is derived, not declared.** Every corpus record is put into an independence class. Two records share a class when they share a `record_id`, a `source_id`, a non-empty `independent_lineage_root_id`, or byte-identical non-empty content, or when a `Quotes`, `Republishes`, or `Supersedes` lineage edge links them. `Supports` edges do not join classes. A class is *anchored* if it holds at least one record that is not a syndicated reprint. An event's `independent_sources` is the number of distinct anchored classes among its members. Classes made only of reprints, with no original identified in the corpus, are reported as `unanchored_reprint_classes` and are not counted as independent. `declared_lineage_roots` keeps the number of distinct declared roots. It is an upper bound that holds only if the declarations are correct. Because declarations can only merge classes, a reprint that declares its own root cannot add an independent source if it is linked to its original or copies its bytes. Member records with an empty declared root are refused (`EmptyField`).
5. **Counts are separate and are not rates.** Each `EventSourceCounts` reports articles (distinct `record_id`), article versions, syndicated-reprint versions, superseded versions, entries, derived independent sources, unanchored reprint classes, declared lineage roots, and per-category entries. The region reports distinct articles, distinct anchored and unanchored classes across included events, and the number of events touching each development kind. `RegionalCoverage::corpus_candidates_untyped` counts corpus event candidates the analyst did not type for this account. No field divides a count by time or presents article volume as how often incidents happen or a crime rate.
6. **Legal process keeps every stage.** Arrests, charges, and judgments must name a `legal_matter_id`. No other category may name one. For each matter, stages are ordered arrest → charge → judgment, with a stable chronological order inside each stage. Each stage carries its reported time and whether its source record has been superseded. A later judgment never removes an earlier arrest or a superseded charge. If a later stage has a reported start before an earlier stage's reported start, `chronology_conflict` is set. The order is kept and the contradiction is surfaced, not resolved. Matters are built per event. A matter id that appears in more than one included event is listed in `RegionalAccount::legal_matters_spanning_events`, and each event keeps its own stages. `latest_reported_stage` and `judgment_reported` describe what was reported. They are not findings made by OIA.
7. **Conflicts and uncertainty survive grouping.** `EventConflictInput` entries must reference member versions, and a correction-resolved conflict must name the correcting versions. The kernel returns them unchanged. Location basis (polygon, point with radius, gazetteer centroid, not reported) and a finite positive uncertainty are validated per entry. Each event shows whether any gazetteer-centroid approximation is present, plus its tightest and widest uncertainty. Superseded records, `needs_reassessment` flags, and non-`Reviewed` statuses stay visible on entries and events.
8. **Window coverage is explicit.** Reported times are strict `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ` strings, and the original strings are kept. Field ranges are checked, and leap seconds are rejected. An event is included if its region matches and at least one entry overlaps the window or has no reported time. An included event is shown whole, and each entry is marked `ReportedBounds`, `UncertaintyOnly` (overlaps only after widening by the stated uncertainty), `Outside`, or `TimeNotReported`. An event whose entries all have no reported time is therefore included in every window for its region; `entries_time_not_reported` makes this visible. `RegionalCoverage` counts events outside the region or window, uncertainty-only and unreported-time entries, entries needing reassessment, and entries not yet reviewed.
9. **Bounded and cancellable.** `RegionalLimits` sets non-zero budgets for events, candidates, members per event, conflicts per event, passages per candidate, and text bytes. Each event's `additional_member_version_ids` and each conflict's version lists are checked against the members-per-event budget, and each candidate's `supporting_passage_ids` against the passages budget, before any per-item lookup. Repeated ids in those lists are refused with `DuplicateIdentifier`. Vectors grow with fallible reservation. The caller's control callback runs before corpus verification, inside it, and inside every loop over events, candidates, member and conflict lists, corpus records, and lineage edges. If it stops the run, the kernel returns `Stopped` with no partial account. Ordering is deterministic: entries by reported start (unreported last), then category, then candidate id. Events by earliest start, then event id.

## Not implemented and known limits

This slice does not include live news or feed acquisition (#13, #17), translation, automatic or proximity-based clustering, persistence, application services, UI, or integrated `A06`/`A19` acceptance. Region ids are caller-declared strings; the kernel checks no geometry and has no region or gazetteer source. Development kinds, legal matter ids, and stated purposes are analyst typing supplied as input. The kernel does not extract them from text.

- **Purpose passages are not checked textually.** The kernel checks that a purpose's passage exists in the candidate's own record. Whether that passage actually states the purpose, and who it attributes it to, is the analyst's typing decision.
- **Independence classes are conservative.** Content matching is exact bytes only, so a lightly edited reprint with no lineage edge and a different source still forms its own class (counted as unanchored if it is marked as a reprint). Joining on `source_id` treats two records from one source as one class.
- **Class derivation is quadratic** in the number of corpus records. The corpus is already bounded by `AssessmentLimits`, and the control callback runs once per record.
- **Shared calendar arithmetic.** `regional.rs` and `verify_corpus` now delegate civil-date and RFC 3339 UTC-second validation to [`oia-time-geo`](shared-time-geo.md) ([#12](https://github.com/mESmaC/oia/issues/12)).

## Fixture example

[`examples/build-a06-regional-account.rs`](../../crates/oia-news-claims/examples/build-a06-regional-account.rs) types the seven synthetic `A06` candidates and groups them by the fixture's `cluster-rev-2` split decision. It then checks the account against `fixtures/news/a06/manifest.json`. The harbor event must give 7 articles, 8 versions, and 3 independent sources. The CX-9 event must give 1/1/1. The region must give 2 proposed events, 8 distinct articles, and 4 independent sources. The derived counts match the manifest, with no unanchored reprint classes. Any mismatch makes the example exit with code 1. The region id, development kinds, legal matter id, and purposes in the example are declared for the example only. The fixture does not contain them.

## Targeted tests

[`tests/regional_contract.rs`](../../crates/oia-news-claims/tests/regional_contract.rs) adds five tests over a small synthetic corpus:

- A syndicated reprint adds an article and a version but no independent source. Legal stages given out of order come back as arrest → charge → judgment, and the superseded charge is still present.
- An observation that reports movement cannot carry a mission. The same purpose on an attributed statement is accepted and attributed to its speaker. An attributed statement without a speaker is rejected.
- Window coverage shows an uncertainty-only entry, an entry with no reported time, and an event excluded as outside the window.
- Two reprints that declare their own lineage roots: the one linked to its original by a `Republishes` edge adds no independent source, and the one with no identified original is counted as an unanchored reprint class. Declared roots (4) and derived independent sources (2) are reported separately. A member record with an empty declared root is refused.
- A duplicated or over-budget `additional_member_version_ids` list is refused before any member is looked up.

A unit test in `regional.rs` checks the UTC parser against known Unix instants and rejects an invalid leap day, month, hour, leap second, and non-`Z` offset.
