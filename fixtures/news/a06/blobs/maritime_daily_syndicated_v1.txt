Harbor Operations Held Near Vesper Harbor; Wire Dispatch Cites 14 Vessels

KESTREL ATOLL (SYNTH-PWS Reprint) -- Harbor operations at fictional Vesper Harbor were disrupted Friday morning after an unidentified surface craft triggered a channel hold near West Fairway Buoy B3 while calibration tenders departed for offshore waters.

According to cooperative watch logs reviewed by Pacific Wire Service, 14 vessels were involved in the West Fairway hold near Buoy B3. Duty pilot Capt. M. Kealoha stated that harbor control held inbound pilotage for 35 minutes while the craft was escorted clear. An unnamed dockside contractor alleged a link to Calibration Box CX-9, which authorities have not confirmed.
