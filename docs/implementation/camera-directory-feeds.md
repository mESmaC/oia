# Camera directory registration, viewing-cone geometry, frame freshness, and bounded clip-retention kernel

Implemented slice of [#28](https://github.com/mESmaC/oia/issues/28), supporting
`CAM-01`, `CAM-02`, `CAM-04`, `docs/architecture.md` §§3.2, 4.3, 4.4, 8, and the
deterministic source-admission and buffer-expiry subset of `A09` in
[the product specification](../spec.md) and [delivery plan](../delivery.md).
The Rust entry points live in [`crates/oia-media/src/camera_directory.rs`](../../crates/oia-media/src/camera_directory.rs):

- `oia_media::camera_directory::CameraDirectoryLimits`
- `oia_media::camera_directory::CameraDirectoryRegistry`
- `oia_media::camera_directory::RegisteredCameraEntry`
- `oia_media::camera_directory::ViewingConeGeometry`
- `oia_media::camera_directory::ClassifiedCameraFrame`
- `oia_media::camera_directory::RetainedCameraClipManifest`

## Contract

1. **One domain model across `oia_media::camera_directory` and `oia_media::camera_anomaly` (`AGENTS.md` §4):**
   - `camera_directory` directly imports and re-exports [`CameraAccessStatus`], [`CameraCalibrationState`], [`CameraHealthState`], [`CameraSourceProfile`], [`CameraViewKind`], [`ContextWindowStatus`], [`FeedObservationKind`], [`FrameArtifactOrigin`], [`MAX_CLOCK_UNCERTAINTY_MS`], [`RESTRICTED_SOURCE_POLICY_NOTICE`], [`RecordedHealthEvent`], [`RetainedFrameRef`], and [`SourceUsePermission`] from [`crate::camera_anomaly`](../../crates/oia-media/src/camera_anomaly.rs).
   - `FeedObservationKind` in `camera_anomaly` is extended with `PeriodicUpstreamStill`, `StaleStillHold`, and `HistoricalArchiveFrame` alongside `UpstreamCameraFeed`, `StillFallback`, `StreetViewFallback`, and `SyntheticPlaceholder`. `FeedObservationKind::eligible_as_event_evidence()` is a coarse kind-level gate admitting only `UpstreamCameraFeed`, `PeriodicUpstreamStill`, and `HistoricalArchiveFrame` (subject to per-frame freshness, archive-window, and explicit `source_capture_time_ms` verification in `ClassifiedCameraFrame::eligible_as_event_evidence()` and `to_retained_frame_ref()`); `FeedObservationKind::eligible_for_live_anomaly_monitoring()` admits only `UpstreamCameraFeed` and `PeriodicUpstreamStill`.
   - Vertical-datum / altitude primitives belong to `oia-time-geo` (`#12`) and are not duplicated in `oia-media`. `CameraHorizontalPosition` records horizontal WGS84 coordinates with explicit uncertainty (`KnownWgs84`), labeled approximate gazetteer/city centroids (`ApproximateGazetteerCentroid`), or `Unknown { reason }` (never `(0.0, 0.0)`).
2. **Camera directory registration, ordinary access, and privacy exclusions (`CAM-01`, `CAM-02`, `docs/architecture.md` §4.4):**
   - `CameraDirectoryRegistry::register_camera` catalogs cameras reachable through ordinary access (`OrdinaryAccessMethod { ExistingDirectoryListing, PublishedViewingLink, SupportedEmbed, SupportedFeedOrApi }`) and attaches `DIRECTORY_LISTING_PROVENANCE_NOTICE` stating that a directory listing does not by itself establish live stream availability or historical archive access.
   - `IntentionalPublicationStatus { EstablishedByOwner, NotEstablishedOrdinaryAccess }` is recorded as provenance metadata only and never refuses or degrades an otherwise ordinarily accessible camera (`CAM-01`, `AGENTS.md` §6).
   - Home interiors (`CameraViewKind::HomeInterior`, `CameraViewKind::ViewIntoHomeInterior`) are unconditionally refused (`CameraDirectoryError::HomeInteriorExcluded`).
   - `CameraViewKind::ResidentialExteriorStreetView` requires `PrivacyViewAssessment::RequiresMaskZones` with non-empty, unique `PrivacyMaskZone`s (`CameraDirectoryError::ResidentialExteriorRequiresMaskZones`). Unmasked zones (`mask_applied == false`), `PrivacyViewAssessment::ExcludedPrivateActivity`, or `SourceLifecycleHealth::DisabledByPolicy` can be cataloged to record the assessment, but block `ingest_frame`, `extract_retained_clip_manifest`, and `RegisteredCameraEntry::to_anomaly_source_profile()` (`CameraDirectoryError::UnmaskedPrivacyZone`, `CameraDirectoryError::PrivateActivityViewExcluded`, `CameraDirectoryError::CameraDisabledByPolicy`).
   - Catalog-only listings (`StreamCapability::None`, `StillRefreshCapability::None`, `ArchiveCapability::NoArchiveEstablished`, or `automated_processing` / `clip_retention` set to `Unknown` or `Prohibited`) are accepted without fabricating stream or archive capabilities; `to_anomaly_source_profile()` returns `Err(CameraDirectoryError::CatalogOnlyCameraHasNoLiveFeed)` when neither stream nor periodic-still capability is declared rather than mislabeling a catalog-only entry as `StillFallback`.
3. **Restricted-source cutoff and explicit re-registration (`CAM-01`, `A09`, `docs/architecture.md` §4.3):**
   - Source lifecycle health (`SourceLifecycleHealth { Unconfigured, Connecting, Available, Degraded, Stale, UnavailableRestricted, DisabledByPolicy }`) is tracked via `record_health_transition`.
   - Recording `SourceLifecycleHealth::UnavailableRestricted` or `Some(CameraHealthState::SourceRestricted)` transitions `access_status` to `CameraAccessStatus::RestrictedOrUnavailable`, appends an open `(restricted_at_ms, None)` interval to `restriction_intervals`, and halts frame ingestion (`CameraDirectoryError::SourceRestricted` with `RESTRICTED_SOURCE_POLICY_NOTICE`).
   - Once restricted, all subsequent `ingest_frame` calls return `CameraDirectoryError::SourceRestricted`, and all subsequent `record_health_transition` retries return `CameraDirectoryError::RestrictedSourceRequiresExplicitReregistration`. Only an explicit `reregister_ordinarily_accessible_version` call with a new, unseen `VersionId` on the **same** `listing_reference` (after that listing returns to ordinary public access) can re-admit the camera while closing the restriction interval `(restricted_at_ms, Some(reregistered_at_ms))`, preserving `prior_source_versions`, health transition history, and per-frame `source_version` provenance, and resetting live and substitute sequence/still session state for the new `source_version`.
4. **Viewing-cone geometry and movement invalidation (`CAM-02`, `CAM-05`, `docs/architecture.md` §§3.2, 8):**
   - `compute_viewing_cone(camera_id, range_m, arc_vertex_count)` validates `range_m` (`(0.0, MAX_VIEWING_CONE_RANGE_M]` where `MAX_VIEWING_CONE_RANGE_M = 100_000.0` m) and `arc_vertex_count` (`MIN_VIEWING_CONE_ARC_VERTICES..=MAX_VIEWING_CONE_ARC_VERTICES`, `2..=128`) before allocating.
   - Projects a spherical horizontal viewing cone on `EARTH_MEAN_RADIUS_M` (`6_371_008.8` m) only when the camera has `CameraHorizontalPosition::KnownWgs84` with non-polar latitude (`|latitude_deg| <= MAX_NON_POLAR_CONE_LATITUDE_DEG`, `89.9999°`; degenerate polar apexes return `CameraDirectoryError::PolarApexUnsupportedForViewingCone`).
   - Distinguishes `ViewingConeClass::CalibratedFieldOfView` (`is_calibrated() == true`, pinned `calibration_version` and `max_angular_error_mdeg`) from `ViewingConeClass::IllustrativeReportedBearing` (`is_calibrated() == false`, with `bearing_uncertainty_deg`), projects both nominal `arc_vertices_lat_lon_deg()` and `uncertainty_widened_arc_vertices_lat_lon_deg()` from the nominal apex across `uncertainty_widened_horizontal_fov_deg()` (widened by calibration/bearing angular uncertainty plus `asin(horizontal_uncertainty_m / range_m)`, clamped to `180°` when `horizontal_uncertainty_m >= range_m`), and refuses unknown or movement-invalidated orientations (`CameraDirectoryError::OrientationUnavailableForViewingCone`).
   - Recording `Some(CameraHealthState::CameraRepositioned)` automatically transitions the camera's orientation to `CameraViewingOrientation::InvalidatedByMovement`, preserving prior orientations in `prior_orientations()` (bounded by `max_health_transitions_per_camera`) and mapping to `CameraCalibrationState::InvalidatedByMovement` in `to_anomaly_source_profile()`. Operators can subsequently recalibrate a repositioned camera via `update_camera_orientation`, which also appends the replaced orientation to `prior_orientations()`.
5. **Frame classification, periodic-still freshness, and archive window verification (`CAM-02`, `AGENTS.md` §2):**
   - `ingest_frame` refuses restricted cameras (`SourceRestricted`), policy-disabled cameras (`CameraDisabledByPolicy`), and cameras where `automated_processing != SourceUsePermission::Permitted` (`AutomatedProcessingNotPermitted`).
   - `ingest_frame` validates 64-character lowercase hex SHA-256 digests, `byte_len >= 1`, `FrameArtifactOrigin::OriginalRetainedFrame` (rejecting `PredictiveOrGeneratedDerivative`), `clock_uncertainty_ms <= MAX_CLOCK_UNCERTAINTY_MS` (`86_400_000` ms = 24 h), three separate `(ingestion_time_ms, sequence_number)` spaces for live/still observations, historical archive frames, and non-evidentiary substitutes, non-regressing live `source_capture_time_ms`, and live-stream capture-age bounds (`LiveFrameExceedsStalenessBound`).
   - `PeriodicUpstreamStill` frames are evaluated against the camera's declared `stale_after_ms` threshold:
     - When `source_capture_time_ms` is present and both capture age and unchanged hold duration are within `stale_after_ms`, the frame is classified as `FrameFreshnessEvaluation::FreshPeriodicStill` (`eligible_as_event_evidence() == true`).
     - When `source_capture_time_ms` is `None` (and hold duration has not exceeded `stale_after_ms`), the frame is classified as `FrameFreshnessEvaluation::PeriodicStillCaptureTimeUnknown` (`eligible_as_event_evidence() == false`).
     - When capture age or unchanged digest/timestamp hold duration exceeds `stale_after_ms`, the frame is downgraded to `FeedObservationKind::StaleStillHold` (`FrameFreshnessEvaluation::StalePeriodicStill`, `eligible_as_event_evidence() == false`), omitted from the retention ring buffer, and camera health transitions to `SourceLifecycleHealth::Stale`.
   - `ClassifiedCameraFrame::to_retained_frame_ref()` requires both `effective_kind.eligible_as_event_evidence()` and an explicit `source_capture_time_ms` (`CameraDirectoryError::MissingSourceCaptureTimeForEvidence`), never fabricating `capture_time_ms` from `ingestion_time_ms`.
   - `HistoricalArchiveFrame` submissions require `ArchiveCapability::VerifiedArchiveWindow`, a matching `archive_version`, and `source_capture_time_ms` within `[earliest_capture_ms, latest_capture_ms]`. Verified archive frames are eligible as retrospective observation evidence (`to_retained_frame_ref()`) without entering the live ring buffer or advancing live sequence state.
   - Non-observation substitutes (`StillFallback`, `StreetViewFallback`, `SyntheticPlaceholder`, `StaleStillHold`) are classified as `FrameFreshnessEvaluation::NonEvidentiarySubstitute`, never enter the ring buffer, never advance live sequence or capture-time state, and return `Err(CameraDirectoryError::NonEvidentiaryFrameCannotBecomeEvidence)` from `to_retained_frame_ref()`.
6. **Bounded retention ring buffer, deterministic FIFO eviction, and clip manifest handoff (`CAM-04`, `A09`, `docs/architecture.md` §8):**
   - Only live evidentiary frames with an explicit `source_capture_time_ms` (`UpstreamCameraFeed` and `FreshPeriodicStill`) on cameras with `clip_retention == SourceUsePermission::Permitted` enter the per-camera ring buffer, stamped with the acquisition `source_version`.
   - Eviction order is strictly deterministic: FIFO by `(ingestion_time_ms, sequence_number, frame_version)`. Expired frames (`age_ms > max_retention_window_ms`) and over-capacity frames (`max_buffered_frames_per_camera`, `max_buffered_bytes_per_camera`) are evicted from the front of the queue and tallied in `RingBufferEvictionSummary` (including window-scoped `evicted_counts_in_window`, which returns `(expired_by_window_in_span, evicted_by_capacity_in_span, eviction_ledger_truncated)` so rolled-off eviction records are explicitly flagged as a lower bound rather than presented as exact).
   - Live sequence gaps (`sequence_number > prev_sequence + 1`) are recorded in bounded `FrameDiscontinuitySpan`s tagged with `source_version`.
   - `extract_retained_clip_manifest` refuses `DisabledByPolicy` cameras (`CameraDisabledByPolicy`) and requires `clip_retention == SourceUsePermission::Permitted` (`CameraDirectoryError::ClipRetentionNotPermitted`; unbuffered cameras use `RegisteredCameraEntry::omitted_retention_context_status()`). It stamps `source_version` from the event's primary frame, filters `pre_context_frames`, `event_frames`, `post_context_frames`, and `discontinuity_spans_in_window` to that `source_version`, and records any excluded cross-version frames (`excluded_cross_version_frames()`), overlapping restriction and policy-disable intervals (`restriction_intervals_in_window()`), coverage gaps exceeding `2 * nominal_interval_ms` across the full `[pre_start_ms, post_end_ms]` window including zero-context requests (`coverage_gaps_in_window()`), and empty context window counts (`empty_context_window_count()`, tracked separately from frame loss counts).
   - `ContextWindowStatus::Complete` is returned **only** when every requested non-zero pre/post context window is populated (`!missing_pre_context && !missing_post_context`), `extracted_at_ms >= post_end_ms`, and there are zero window evictions, zero capacity evictions, zero ledger truncations, zero dropped frames, zero coverage gaps, zero excluded cross-version frames, and zero restriction intervals in `[pre_start_ms, post_end_ms]`. Otherwise `ContextWindowStatus::PartialBufferExpired` is returned.

## Limitations and remaining `#28` scope

- This module is an in-memory directory, geometry, classification, and ring-buffer kernel; it does not perform live network HTTP/RTSP/HLS/WebRTC fetching (`#13`), decode video containers or image codecs, persist camera catalogs or retained clip blobs to `oia-store` (`#11`), schedule background worker jobs (`#14`), or render map/globe viewing cones and camera drawers in the UI (`#15`, `#16`).
- Ingest-triggered transitions of `RegisteredCameraEntry::current_health()` between `SourceLifecycleHealth::Available` and `SourceLifecycleHealth::Stale` update current health state directly; structured `SourceHealthTransition` audit entries (with caller-supplied `transition_id`s and anomaly-fault mappings) are recorded explicitly via `record_health_transition`.
- Vertical-datum / altitude primitives belong to `oia-time-geo` (`#12`).
- No Lean theorem or formal Rust-to-Lean correspondence claim is made for this slice.

Five targeted contract tests in [`crates/oia-media/tests/camera_directory_contract.rs`](../../crates/oia-media/tests/camera_directory_contract.rs) verify:
1. Directory registration with `IntentionalPublicationStatus::NotEstablishedOrdinaryAccess` as non-blocking metadata (`CAM-01`), catalog-only camera listings (`CAM-02`, `CatalogOnlyCameraHasNoLiveFeed`), unconditional home-interior refusal (`HomeInterior`, `ViewIntoHomeInterior`), required privacy mask zones on `ResidentialExteriorStreetView`, and acquisition + clip-extraction + `to_anomaly_source_profile()` refusal on unmasked zones, `ExcludedPrivateActivity`, `DisabledByPolicy`, or `automated_processing != Permitted`.
2. Calibrated vs. illustrative viewing-cone geometry on the WGS84 sphere (including `uncertainty_widened_horizontal_fov_deg` and `uncertainty_widened_arc_vertices_lat_lon_deg`), polar-apex refusal (`PolarApexUnsupportedForViewingCone`), automatic orientation invalidation upon `CameraRepositioned`, subsequent recalibration via `update_camera_orientation` with preserved `prior_orientations()`, and pre-allocation bounds checks on cone range and vertex count.
3. Periodic-still freshness evaluation against `stale_after_ms` (`FreshPeriodicStill`, `PeriodicStillCaptureTimeUnknown`, and downgrade to `StaleStillHold`), refusal to fabricate `capture_time_ms` when `source_capture_time_ms` is `None` (`MissingSourceCaptureTimeForEvidence`), live-frame staleness bounds (`LiveFrameExceedsStalenessBound`), non-evidentiary substitute isolation (`StillFallback`, `StreetViewFallback`, `SyntheticPlaceholder`) in its own sequence space, verified historical archive window checks with independent archive sequence tracking, and generated-frame / clock-uncertainty refusals.
4. Permanent restricted-source cutoff (`SourceRestricted`) blocking both frame ingestion and automatic retry transitions until explicit `reregister_ordinarily_accessible_version` on the same `listing_reference` with a new `VersionId`, preserving per-frame `source_version` provenance, `restriction_intervals`, and `excluded_cross_version_frames` across re-registration.
5. Deterministic FIFO `(ingestion_time_ms, sequence_number, frame_version)` ring-buffer eviction by capacity and retention window, window-scoped eviction and discontinuity accounting, `eviction_ledger_truncated` lower-bound flagging, edge/step coverage gap detection (`coverage_gaps_in_window`), `RetainedCameraClipManifest` context-completeness rules (`Complete` vs. `PartialBufferExpired`), and direct handoff into `CameraAnomalyMonitor`.
