      Accounting limits
      Publishers are declared source IDs, not independent witnesses. Shared origin IDs mark declared common sourcing, not proven independence. Proposed event IDs are grouping suggestions, not verified incidents. Neither article volume nor group count is an incident frequency or crime rate.
      
        
      Time and corrections
      Publication, update and event times are separate imported strings; OIA does not normalize, sort or infer freshness from them. Correction, retraction and availability are imported declarations. Missing text or an unavailable page does not establish retraction. All versions stay retained; dependent-report invalidation and durable cases remain unimplemented.

      Limits
      Exact, case-sensitive search within single lines, up to 200 matches shared across a corpus search; a file the limit leaves unsearched is reported as such, never as empty. Zero matches cover only the searched bytes. Byte ranges are zero-based and end-exclusive. Hidden controls are escaped for display and in copied citation text. A hash shows byte identity, not authenticity; a corpus slot is the selection order, not a source or version identity, and identical files in two slots are one set of bytes, not corroboration. A valid citation is a structural anchor in this session's retained bytes, not a source-version citation, entailment or review. Publication, author, and event time are not recorded. Changes on disk do not change a snapshot.

        Limits
        The case and the camera directory are bundled synthetic fixtures; nothing drawn is an observation. Points, rings and regions come only from recorded geometry; a record without geometry is listed, not placed. A camera wedge is an illustrative bearing from a numeric tag, never a calibrated field of view, and a camera without a tag has no wedge. Imagery is a live request to the registered sources: the basemap for the layer and UTC day shown, and, when zoomed in, finer detail tiers that are other capture periods (a yearly Sentinel-2 composite, United States aerial orthoimagery). None shows the case period, and a missing or unpublished tile is a coverage gap. Tile counts are what this renderer observed in this session. No terrain, labels, roads or tracks are drawn, because none is registered. Camera position is a view setting, not a measurement. Analyst annotations are notes drawn in this session: they are not observations, never change a case record, are measured on a sphere, and are not saved when OIA closes. Persistent cases, clustering, footprints and live directory queries are not in this build.

        Kernel limitations
          
        
          Limits
          Cues are investigative diagnostics from the retained bytes: no cue proves camera origin, and no absence of cues proves an image is unedited or not generated. The assessment is always inconclusive and unreviewed. Learned AI detection and credential or watermark verification are not implemented. Decoded samples are derivatives (encoded raster coordinates, 8-bit RGBA, no colour management). The image is displayed in its encoded orientation so that drawn regions share the report's coordinates; an EXIF orientation tag is applied neither here nor in the kernel. Every JPEG report is partial: entropy completeness is unverified and decoder recovery is possible. Block candidates are exact grid-aligned repeats, which ordinary flat regions also produce. The original is never modified; no residual map is written by this instrument.
