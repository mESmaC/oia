PACIFIC WIRE SERVICE DISPATCH: PWS-2026-1015-884 (VERSION 1)
PUBLISHED: 2026-10-15T21:05:00Z
DATELINE: HONOLULU (Oahu regional centroid approx. 21.4389N 158.0001W, gazetteer centroid, WGS84)

HONOLULU (PWS) -- Harbor operations west of Honolulu were disrupted Thursday morning after an unidentified surface craft triggered a channel hold near Barbers Point while naval range ships departed for offshore waters.

According to cooperative watch logs reviewed by Pacific Wire Service, 14 vessels were involved in the West Approach Channel standoff as harbor tugs responded near Buoy #3. Duty pilot Capt. M. Kealoha said harbor control held inbound pilotage for 35 minutes while the craft was escorted clear.

An anonymous dockside contractor alleged that the channel closure was ordered to recover a classified military sub-surface payload tied to the offshore naval exercise in Warning Area W-188B. Officials had not responded to the contractor's allegation at press time.
